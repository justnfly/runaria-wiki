# Cooking

Cooking is a profession you *do*, not a menu you click. You buy stations, place them in your own
kitchen, gather produce, and work each dish through the stations it needs. A finished plate carries
your name, your Cooking level and the quality of everything that went into it — so two players
making the same recipe do not get the same item.

!!! tip "Start with Kate"
    **Kate** is Sylari's chef, in the farmhouse and bakery she shares with Keidi. Talk to her to get
    started — she'll explain the stations, sell you your first one, and tell you what's worth cooking
    at your level. Everything on these pages, she can walk you through in person.

## The short version

1. **Buy a station** from the Cooking section of the server shop, and place it wherever you like.
2. **Gather ingredients** — crops, animal produce, and pantry staples from the shop.
3. **Cook.** Each dish is made at one particular station.
4. **Eat it, or sell it.** Cooked food is traded through the market, and good food sells.

And when you're ready to pour instead of plate: **[Brewing](brewing.md)** makes drinks with no
recipes at all, and **[Alchemy](alchemy.md)** turns the same knowledge into potions. Food, drink
and potion buffs all stack — the fully-buffed run uses all three.

## What a dish gives you

Every dish restores **hunger**, **saturation**, **health** and **mana**. How much depends on its
rarity — a Common dish restores less than a basic health potion, while the top of the ladder is in a
different league entirely.

On top of that, dishes grant **timed stat buffs**:

| Rarity | Buffs |
| --- | --- |
| **Common** | One stat, briefly |
| **Uncommon** | One stat, for longer |
| **Rare** | Sometimes two stats, or one that runs much longer |
| **Epic** | Same shape as Rare, but stronger and longer — plus a Saturation effect |
| **Legendary** | Always two stats |
| **Unique** | Any number of stats, and very hard to make |

The stats themselves span the whole roster — health, mana, damage, defence, crit, movement speed,
regeneration and more. A few dishes carry something rarer still: effects that boost **profession
experience**, **class experience**, **dungeon party experience**, or the **quality of traits** you
roll, for half an hour at a time. Those are worth saving for the run you actually care about.

## The cook is baked into the dish

This is the part that makes a good chef valuable.

!!! note "Whoever eats it gets *your* numbers"
    A dish's strength is calculated from the **cook's Cooking level at the moment it was made**, and
    then stored in the item. A meal from a master chef is exactly as strong in a brand-new player's
    hands as it is in theirs.

Which is the whole argument for having a dedicated cook in your guild — and for buying food from one
rather than making your own while you're still levelling.

The item itself tells you the truth: every dish is stamped with who prepared it and at what level,
and its lore lists the real numbers *that plate* will give you.

## Ingredient quality

Crops come in three grades, and cooking carries that grade all the way through to the plate.

| Ingredient | Dish becomes | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal produce | Ordinary | Baseline |
| **Silver Star** | **Amazing** | Stronger |
| **Golden Star** | **Perfection** | Strongest |

Quality is proportional, not all-or-nothing — cooking with *some* starred produce lands you part of
the way. It also survives processing: dice a golden-star crop and the result is still golden, so a
batter made from good produce bakes into a better cake.

Some recipes go further and **demand** a grade. A handful of Legendary and Unique dishes will not
accept anything below Silver or Golden star, which is what makes them projects rather than recipes.
See [Farming](../lifeskills/farming.md#star-quality) for how to get starred crops reliably.

## Levelling

Cooking is one of the ten [professions](../progression/professions.md), and it levels from cooking —
plus a trickle from harvesting crops, so farmers gain it without trying.

Level does three things:

- **Unlocks recipes.** Rare needs 15, Epic 35, Legendary 65, Unique 100.
- **Strengthens everything you cook**, in both effect size and duration.
- **Sharpens the timer** at the stove and oven. Below level 10 you get a progress bar and nothing
  else; at 25 the last digit appears; at 60 the seconds are readable; at 100 you see the full
  countdown. Early cooks learn timing by feel, and veterans read the clock.
