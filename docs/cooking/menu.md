# The Menu

Every dish in Runaria — **120** of them, across four stations. This page is the catalogue:
what exists, what it's worth, and where it's made. It deliberately does **not** list ingredients.
Finding those out is what the [Recipe Book](recipe-book.md) is for.

!!! tip "New to cooking?"
    Talk to **Kate** in Sylari. She'll set you up with a station and point you at your first recipes.

## How to read this

Rarity decides how much a dish restores, how strong its buff is, and how long that buff runs.
The level shown is the **Cooking level** you need before the recipe will work at all.

| Rarity | Cooking level | Dishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Common** | None | 30 |
| **Uncommon** | None | 30 |
| **Rare** | 15 | 25 |
| **Epic** | 35 | 16 |
| **Legendary** | 65 | 14 |
| **Unique** | 100 | 5 |


## Common

Everyday food. Most of it needs no Cooking level at all, so this is where everybody starts.

*No level requirement.*

**Prep Station** — Cabbage Tomato Wrap, Garden Sandwich, Garden Veggie Skewer, Garlic Corn Bowl, Onigiri  
**Chopping Station** — Banana Shake, Carrot Juice, Chili Berry Sauce, Grape Cucumber Jam Toast, Orange Juice, Strawberry, Tomato Shake  
**Kitchen Stove** — Chinese Cabbage Stir Fry, Cooked Frog Leg, Fried Egg, Roasted Corn Pepper Mix  
**Kitchen Oven** — Chocolate Cake, Chocolate Cupcake, Chocolate Ring, Chocolate Roll, Chocolate Slab, Cocoa Drop, Cocoa Macaroon, Crusty Loaf, Glazed Donut, Honey Loaf, Lemon Drop, Plum Drop, Strawberry Drop, Toast  

## Uncommon

Still open to a beginner, but a step up in what it restores and how long its buff lasts.

*No level requirement.*

**Prep Station** — Bacon Sandwich, Salmon Nigiri  
**Kitchen Stove** — Beer, Cooked Bacon, Cooked Chicken Thigh, Cooked Sausage, French Fries, Glow Berry Soup, Hop Braised Garden Stew, Pasta, Soup, Takoyaki, Warped Fungus Stew, Wine  
**Kitchen Oven** — Baguette, Chocolate Bar, Chocolate Donut, Chocolate Ice Cream, Cream Pudding, Dango, Honey Toast, Melonpan, Milk Loaf, Mochi, Olive Flatbread, Pancake, Peach Cake, Strawberry Cake, Strawberry Roll, Waffle  

## Rare

The first real gate. Two stats sometimes, or one that lasts noticeably longer.

*Requires Cooking 15.*

**Prep Station** — Berry Honey Yogurt Bowl, Burger, Cucumber Pepper Salad, Kebab, Sushi, Taco  
**Kitchen Stove** — Cherry Garden Porridge, Cooling Cucumber Soup, Gyoza, Gyudon, Karaage, Tonkatsu, Yakitori  
**Kitchen Oven** — Blackberry Jelly, Blueberry Cake, Blueberry Drop, Blueberry Ice Cream, Cherry Cake, Cherry Roll, Crossaint, Fish Pastry, Honey Waffle, Pancake with Chocolate, Pancake with Honey, Plum Cucumber Cake Slice  

## Epic

Stronger buffs, longer timers, and the first dishes that grant a Saturation effect on top.

*Requires Cooking 35.*

**Prep Station** — Avocado Rice Bowl, Hotdog, Kiwi Ranger Salad, Papaya Guardian Bowl, Pineapple Avocado Skewer  
**Kitchen Stove** — Eggplant Avocado Stew, Japanese Curry, Okonomiyaki, Shabu Shabu, Tomato Soup  
**Kitchen Oven** — Coconut Mana Pudding, Goji Drop, Muffin, Pizza, Raspberry Cupcake, Raspberry Jelly  

## Legendary

Always two stats, and always worth the walk to a good kitchen. Several want starred produce.

*Requires Cooking 65.*

**Prep Station** — Lemon Avocado Fish Plate  
**Kitchen Stove** — Blueberry Cucumber Tea, Mango Flame Curry, Orange Avocado Honey Roast  
**Kitchen Oven** — Carambola Jelly, Coconut Macaroon, Guava Jelly, Guava Spirit Tart, Kiwi Roll, Lychee Jelly, Mango Cake, Mango Ice Cream, Papaya Jelly, Raspberry Cucumber Tart  

## Unique

Five dishes, and every one of them is a project. They demand golden-star produce and a master cook.

*Requires Cooking 100.*

**Prep Station** — Carambola Star Feast, Lychee Battle Dessert, Redpacket Golden Banquet  
**Kitchen Stove** — Durian War Stew, Goji Avocado Recovery Tea  

## Prepared ingredients

Four things made at a station aren't dishes at all — they're components other recipes ask for:
**Croissant Dough**, **Pancake Batter**, **Waffle Batter** and **Tomato Paste**. You'll meet them
as soon as you start baking.

!!! note "Quality carries through"
    A batter made from starred produce stays starred, and passes that on to whatever you bake
    with it. See [Cooking](index.md#ingredient-quality).
