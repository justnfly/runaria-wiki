# The Menu

Every dish in Runaria — **120** of them — and exactly what each one does.

This page deliberately lists **no ingredients**. Finding those out is what the
[Recipe Book](recipe-book.md) is for.

!!! tip "New to cooking?"
    Talk to **Kate** in Sylari. She'll set you up with a station and point you at your first recipes.

!!! note "These are base values"
    Every figure below is the dish at **Cooking level 1, made with ordinary produce** — the floor,
    not the ceiling. Your own plate is stronger: effects scale with the cook's level and with the
    star quality of what went in, and the item's own lore always shows the real numbers for *that*
    plate. See [Cooking](index.md#the-cook-is-baked-into-the-dish).

| Rarity | Cooking level | Dishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Common** | None | 30 |
| **Uncommon** | None | 30 |
| **Rare** | 15 | 25 |
| **Epic** | 35 | 16 |
| **Legendary** | 65 | 14 |
| **Unique** | 100 | 5 |

## Prepared ingredients

Four things made at a station aren't dishes — they're components other recipes ask for:
**Croissant Dough**, **Pancake Batter**, **Waffle Batter** and **Tomato Paste**. You'll meet them
as soon as you start baking.

!!! note "Quality carries through"
    A batter made from starred produce stays starred, and passes that on to whatever you bake
    with it. See [Cooking](index.md#ingredient-quality).
