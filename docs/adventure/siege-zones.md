# Siege Zones

Control points scattered across the world. Hold one and it pays you loot on a timer — but everyone inside is fair game.

## Taking a zone

**Capturing an empty zone:** stand in the capture point for **60 seconds**. The first to finish takes it.

**Taking an owned zone:** owned zones **cannot** be timer-captured. Entering one as an enemy wakes the **zone boss** and its minions. Whichever side deals the **most boss damage** takes the zone when it dies. If nobody kills the boss within **15 minutes**, the zone resets to unclaimed instead.

When a zone changes hands, the previous owner's boss and minions are cleared, so new owners never inherit defenders.

## Holding a zone

- After a zone changes hands there's a **60-minute cooldown** before it can flip again.
- A zone **auto-unclaims after 60 minutes** with no owner member online.
- Zone ownership shows on the web map. Boss spawns, captures, and losses are all announced.

## The reward vault

Each zone has a linked chest that becomes a **paginated vault with unlimited storage** — loot accrues into it on a timer while you hold the zone, and never overflows onto the ground.

!!! danger "Collect often"
    When a zone is taken by a **different** owner, the vault is **wiped down to 9 items**. Rare items get first claim on those slots, rarest first, and the rest are topped up with the most recent normal items. Everything else is gone.

    First-time capture of an *unclaimed* zone does not trigger this.

## Commands

There are none — you capture by standing your ground and fighting.

Note that everyone inside a siege zone is attackable regardless of [rogue status](pvp.md), and kills there count toward automatic bounty progression.
