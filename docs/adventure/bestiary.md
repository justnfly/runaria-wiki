# Bestiary

Runaria replaces most of what you'd expect to meet in the world. This is what's out there, where it lives, and what's worth killing.

## Progression at a glance

The server's own experience values rank the fight ladder like this:

**Enderling → Ender Watchman → End Strider → Temple Skeletons → Lich King → Cog Sentinel**

---

## The End frontier

An End-themed area out in the overworld, and the best material farm in the game. This is where new adventurers cut their teeth — **Mest** is stationed there to help.

| Creature | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Enderling** | A small enderman. Fast, teleports constantly. The safe, reliable grind. |
| **End Wraith** | A small flying spirit that dashes and spin-slashes. Usually summoned, occasionally alone. |
| **Ender Watchman** | A sorcerer with a lantern who **summons End Wraiths** and shoves with the lantern. |
| **Ender Crawler** | A multi-legged beast with fang, horn and jump attacks. |
| **Enderbruiser** | A heavy brute that smashes and throws players. |
| **Endermauler** | A large screaming horror that **teleports periodically** and closes with slash flurries. |
| **Ender Strider** | *"Quite slow, but pack a powerful punch."* The prize of the zone. |

### What they drop

Enderlings give Teleport Dust, Deep Pearls and Void Crystals — the reagents behind every gemstone and enchant stone you'll ever craft. Watchmen, Crawlers and Endermaulers add Void Fabric, Ender Carapace, Heart of Ender and rarer void materials.

**End Striders are several times more productive than anything else here**, dropping those same reagents in bulk, and they're the **only source of the Ancient Key** that opens [Temple](dungeons.md#temple).

!!! tip "You can ride one"
    Ender Striders are **tameable**. Feed one a Golden Apple — roughly a one-in-three chance per apple — and it gains a saddle. They carry a passenger, too.

## Temple undead

Found only inside [Temple](dungeons.md#temple).

- **Skeleton Warrior** — *"A weak skeletal grunt. They appear plentifully guarding the dungeon, but no one knows why."*
- **Skeleton Archer** — *"Their pride in their skills is unmatched, even though they miss more shots than they hit."*
- **Skeleton Shaman** — a skeletal caster. The Codex hints these may be what animates the Cog Sentinel.
- **Lich King** — *"He used to command a great army before ending up trapped in the dungeon. No one knows how he got there."* A robed skeletal caster who fights with a scythe and shadow magic.
- **Cog Sentinel** — *"No one knows how this assortment of gears gained sentience."* A towering clockwork guardian that fires tracking projectiles which scorch the floor and detonate. The highest-experience kill on the server.

## The cult

A ritual-themed group you'll only meet somewhere well off the beaten path. If you've run into these, you found something.

- **Cultist** — hits hardest of the group in raw melee.
- **Berserk** — a hulking, slow-swinging brute.
- **Monster** — a fast charging mass.
- **Sorcerer** — mixes melee with ranged spellcasting.
- **Lich** — a floating caster that keeps its distance, panics away from you, and leaves orbs behind.

Cultists and Berserks carry **Dark Soul Sand**; Monsters and Liches carry **Smouldering Ash**. Both are gemcrafting materials you can't get anywhere else.

Something considerably worse than any of them waits at the end.

## The Deep Dark

Sculk creatures spawn naturally in **Deep Dark** biomes. They're capped tightly so you'll meet them one or two at a time.

- **Echo Enshrouder** — the heavyweight. Soul explosions, sculk spikes, blankets the area in darkness, fires from both hands. The rarest of the group.
- **Sculk Sentinel** — a grappler that grabs, smashes, stomps and lashes with its tongue.
- **Sculk Screamer** — **screams the moment it enters combat.** Expect company.
- **Sculked Crustacean** and **Sculk Crabby** — pinching swarm creatures.
- **Soul Bearer** — a ranged shooter.

!!! warning "They drop nothing"
    None of the sculk creatures drop anything at all. Fight them to clear a path or for the challenge, not for profit.

## Ocean life

A full marine ecosystem spawns across the oceans, split by water temperature.

**Gentle Ocean** — Catfish, Dolphin, Jellyfish, Lobster, Manatee, Manta Ray, Sea Slug, Seabass, **Shark**, Spider Crab, Starfish, Sunfish, **Swordfish**

**Exotic Seas** (warm and lukewarm) — Betta Fish, Blue Glaucus, Clownfish, Crab, Dory Fish, Electric Eel, Garden Eel, Lionfish, Mahi Mahi, Mantis Shrimp, **Narwhal**, Oarfish, Ocean Snail, Seahorse, Tropical Fish

**Dangerous Deeps** (cold, frozen and deep) — **Anglerfish**, **Barracuda**, **Box Jellyfish**, **Giant Squid**, **Hammerhead Shark**, Nautilus, **Octopus**, **Orca**, Pearl Clam, **Piranha**, **Pistol Shrimp**, Pufferfish, Sea Angel, **Stingray**

Bold entries bite back. Sharks, Orcas and Hammerheads are the apex predators — but the **Pistol Shrimp** is the sleeper threat, tiny and far harder-hitting than anything its size.

Worth hunting: Nautilus for Nautilus Shells, **Pearl Clam** for diamonds, gold and iron, Octopus for Ink Sacs in bulk, Blue Glaucus for Experience Bottles, Manta Ray for Phantom Membrane.

## The Menagerie

A realistic-animal ecosystem spawns across the overworld by biome, and it's the backbone of Runaria's hunting quests. **Every one of them is peaceful and deals no damage — with one exception.**

| Where | Animals |
| --- | --- |
| **Savanna** | Antelope, Elephant *(spawns as a parade of four)*, Giraffe, Hippo, Leopard, Rhino, Zebra, Budgies in ten colours |
| **Snowy Plains** | Snow Leopard, Reindeer, Blue Tit, Cardinal, Robin |
| **Jungle** | Chameleon, Koala |
| **Swamp** | Frogs, Capybara |
| **Plains** | Turkey, Capybara |
| **Desert** | Jerboa |
| **Taiga & Old Growth Pine** | Hedgehog |
| **Birch Forest** | Grizzly Bear |
| **Beach** | Flamingo |
| **Frozen Ocean** | Penguin — the most common of the lot by a wide margin |
| **Indoors, anywhere** | **Rats** — the only animal that spawns *inside* buildings, and they do it often |

!!! danger "The Black Widow"
    Small, venomous, bites on contact, and deliberately **unrestricted — it can turn up in any biome**. The only hostile animal in the Menagerie.

### Trophies

Every animal drops experience plus a species trophy. Common animals give theirs often; the rare ones are worth hunting deliberately.

**Easy and frequent** — Capybara Pelt, Frog Eyes, Hedgehog Quills, Turkey Feathers, Zebra Pelt, Penguin Feathers

**Worth chasing** — **Rhino Horn** (the rarest at roughly 1 in 20), Jerboa Tail, Reindeer Horn, Giraffe Pelt, Orca Fin, Rat Tail

**Seahorses** are the odd one out: they drop their fin almost every time *and* a full spread of live corals, making them the easiest coral farm on the server.

## Odds and ends

**Snails** are harmless, extremely slow critters that squelch when they move, with nine randomised shell colours and slightly randomised size — no two look quite alike. They're very rare, drop nothing, and exist purely as ambience.

**Some vanilla mobs hit harder here.** Cave Spiders, Wither Skeletons and Giants have boosted health and damage server-wide.

**Time of day doesn't matter.** No custom creature on Runaria has a day/night, weather or moon-phase spawn condition.
