# Dungeons & Parties

Runaria has two dungeons open right now: **Temple**, a private instanced run, and **Haunted**, a walk-in world dungeon. Both feed first-clear and speedrun leaderboards, and **neither has been cleared yet** — every First Clear slot is still open.

## Two kinds of dungeon

**Instanced dungeons** give your group a private copy of the dungeon. You enter by command, the run is yours alone, and it's cleaned up when you finish.

**World Dungeons** are fixed sites out in the world. You walk in — no command. They run one group at a time on a global cooldown shared by everyone, and the site resets in place.

---

## Temple

*The first real dungeon. Instanced, 1–5 players, soloable.*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | Instanced — private copy per group |
| **Players** | 1–5 |
| **Time limit** | 30 minutes |
| **Cooldown** | None — re-run it immediately |
| **Boss** | Cog Sentinel, with the Lich King as a mid-dungeon mini-boss |

### Getting in

Temple is not walked into. It sits behind the **Ancient Portal**, and opening it takes a key:

1. Fight in the **End frontier** until an **End Strider** drops an **Ancient Key** — roughly a 1-in-7 chance, so expect several kills.
2. Take the key to the matching **Ancient Altar** and interact. The altar consumes the key and lights the portal.
3. Walk through. The portal takes you straight in and **closes behind you** — one entry per key.

!!! tip "Where's the portal?"
    Runaria's guides only speak of it in rumours, and we're keeping it that way. Ask around in-game — someone will point you in the right direction.

### The run

You start by clearing skeletons on the entry path. Doors open, the halls fill with archers on the ledges and warriors below, and your objective becomes **Find The Boss**.

Push into the great chamber and it seals behind you. The **Lich King** drops in with a war-band, and a new objective begins: **Find The Lever**. Four levers are placed high around the chamber and only one is correct — and it's **randomised every run**, so nobody can just tell you the answer. Pull a wrong one and more archers arrive.

The correct lever shifts the doors onward. Guard pairs wait along the corridor, and then the **arena** seals you in for a three-wave survival fight, each wave clearing before the next arrives. The last wave is ten mobs at once.

Survive it and the arena opens onto the **Cog Sentinel** — a towering clockwork guardian, and the single most valuable kill on the server for class experience. Kill it and an exit gate spawns.

### Checkpoints

Temple is the only dungeon with **checkpoints**, banked at the lever chamber and again at the arena. Players who fall behind get pulled forward so nobody is stranded behind a sealed door.

### Loot

- **Skeletons** — Scorched Bone every time, Common Manastones about half the time, and roughly a 1-in-12 chance at a Common weapon or armour piece.
- **Lich King** — Broken Ribs, guaranteed Common gear, and a **1-in-4 shot at Common+**.
- **Cog Sentinel** — Rusted Chains, guaranteed Common gear, and a **1-in-4 shot at Common+**.
- **Bone Mimic chests** — the Temple's treasure. Cracked gemstones of all four families, diamonds, gold and iron blocks, or Ember Stones — and each chest **rolls up to three times**. Right-click to open, no key needed.

## Haunted

*The second dungeon. A shared, walk-in sprawl with no time limit and no checkpoints.*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | World Dungeon — walk in, shared with whoever else is inside |
| **Players** | 1–5 |
| **Time limit** | None — take as long as you like |
| **Reset cooldown** | 10 minutes, shared globally |
| **Boss** | Valerius |

`/warp haunted` will get you to the right part of the world. Finding the entrance itself counts as a **Codex discovery** worth 50 XP, so it's worth locating properly.

!!! warning "Something guards the door"
    A **Parasitic Abomination** patrols the surface entrance. It isn't part of the dungeon proper — it's the doorman, and it hits hard. Below half health it starts seeding **Parasitic Buds** around itself that explode when killed *and* on a timer.

### The run

The moment you enter, the halls populate — Monsters and Liches on patrol, Cultist and Berserk pairs, and **eight wooden chests** hidden through the complex. Your objective is **Open The Boss Door**.

**Six hidden buttons** are scattered across the entire dungeon, from the deepest halls to the far west wing. Press all six, in any order, and the boss door opens. Finding them *is* the dungeon — it's a genuine exploration run, and much faster with people splitting up.

Two optional areas are worth your time:

- **The Library** — nine bookshelves, one of which hides a **Dungeon TNT** charge. The others just tell you "not that one."
- **The Treasure Vault** — that TNT blows open a specific wall, revealing a sealed treasure room. It's the only destructible-terrain puzzle on the server.

And one area that is not optional once you walk into it:

- **The Trap Room** — the door slams behind you and you have to **hold the point for 60 seconds** while a Berserk and Sorcerer pair spawns every 15 seconds from seven directions. Survive and the door reopens.

With all six buttons pressed, **Valerius** — a heavily armoured swordsman — spawns in the western boss chamber.

### Loot

- **Monsters and Liches** — Smouldering Ash, guaranteed Common Manastones, and a small chance at Common or Common+ gear.
- **Cultists, Berserks and Sorcerers** — Dark Soul Sand, plus the same Manastone and gear rolls.
- **Valerius** — a guaranteed Skeletal Heart, Uncommon Manastones, and **two rolls** on a Common+/Uncommon table. That's the **best gear-per-kill rate in the game**, and the only reliable source of Uncommon-tier loot.
- **Wooden chests** — cracked gemstones and Common or Common+ armour. One-shot, then they vanish.

!!! note "World Dungeon caveat"
    Traits that shorten dungeon cooldowns don't apply to Haunted — the cooldown is global rather than per-player. Traits that add **loot rolls** still work fine.

## Recommended progression

1. **Start in the End frontier.** Enderlings are a safe grind; **End Striders** are the real prize — far more materials, and the only source of the Ancient Key. Talk to **Mest**, who is stationed there to help newcomers.
2. **Run Temple** with your key. There's no cooldown, so re-run it for gear — you'll need a fresh key each time.
3. **Gear up to Common+** off the Lich King and Cog Sentinel.
4. **Find Haunted.** Pick up **Haunted Contracts from Kael** first so your clearing counts toward bounties, and work toward **The Long Cull** — 40 kills each of five mob types for 60,000 coins.
5. **Bring a group** for Haunted. The trap room is a 60-second hold under continuous spawns.

## Parties

`/party` opens the party GUI.

- Parties hold up to **6 players** and **survive server restarts**.
- Set them **Open** or **Closed**. Browse open parties, or post in **Looking-For-Players** with class and level filters.
- Member entries show class, level, status, and each member's three fastest runs.
- **Party experience sharing:** when any member earns main-class experience from any source, every other online member gets the same full amount, wherever they are. Profession experience is never shared.
- If your party leader starts a dungeon, everyone gets an opt-in prompt. The run launches once everyone accepts, or after 30 seconds.

## Inside any dungeon

- A scoreboard tracks elapsed time, lives, players alive, and your current objective.
- **Lives are shared by the party.** Dying puts you into a spectator state rather than ending the run — the rest fight on.
- **Loot chests are per-player.** Everyone opens the same chest and gets their own roll; chests are never used up.
- Loot that doesn't fit your inventory is **delivered on your next login**.
- Disconnecting gives you **2 minutes** to reconnect.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/dungeon list` | List dungeons and whether each is playable (alias `/dg`) |
| `/dungeon info <id>` | Player range, time limit, cooldown |
| `/dungeon join <name>` | Enter an instanced dungeon — party if you lead one |
| `/dungeon join` | Accept a pending party launch |
| `/dungeon leave` | Leave your run, or teleport out of a World Dungeon |
| `/party` | Party GUI (alias `/p`) |
| `/leaderboard` | First clears and fastest times (alias `/lb`) |

!!! tip "Stuck inside a World Dungeon?"
    `/dungeon leave` teleports you to the site's safe exit.
