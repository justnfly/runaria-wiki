# Ores & Materials

Runaria has its own metal economy sitting on top of vanilla mining. Twenty-eight raw ores refine into twenty-six metals across four tiers, and everything you craft is made of them.

!!! warning "You cannot strip-mine for these"
    **Custom ores do not generate in the world.** No amount of tunnelling will find you Nightsteel. They come from mining nodes, world events, and the market — see below.

## Where ore comes from

### Mining nodes

The main source. **Mine 1 — the Sacred Garden** — is reachable with `/warp mine1`, and three kinds of node spawn there. Nodes are spread out deliberately, so you can't camp one spot.

| Node | What it gives |
| --- | --- |
| **Boulder** | A big pile of one vanilla resource — iron, gold, copper, coal, diamonds, emeralds, redstone, lapis, quartz **or netherite scrap**. Every boulder is a lottery ticket. |
| **Copper Node** | Raw Mystical Molten Alloy, Froststeel or Abyssium |
| **Iron Node** | Raw Nightsteel, Sunsteel or Starsteel |

**You must be holding a pickaxe.** Any other tool tells you "Can't mine with this tool!" and does nothing. Your pickaxe tier decides how many hits a node takes:

| Pickaxe | Hits per ore node |
| --- | --- |
| Wooden | 5 |
| Stone | 4 |
| Iron | 3 |
| Golden | 2 |
| Diamond or Netherite | 1 |

There's a half-second cooldown between registered hits, so **a diamond pickaxe is a massive throughput upgrade** — five times the rate of a wooden one.

More mines are planned for the higher ore tiers.

### Resource Drops

[Ore and Meteor Drops](../adventure/resource-drops.md) crash into the world for 20 minutes at a time and are the **best early way to stockpile Ember Stone**, alongside a spread of tier-1 raw ores.

### The market

Every raw ore is stocked at [the server shop](market.md) if you'd rather buy than mine.

## The four tiers

### Tier 1 — refined at the Basic Refinery

The early-game backbone. Everything a new player crafts is made from these six.

| Ore | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| **Raw Nightsteel** | Deep navy blue |
| **Raw Abyssium** | Dark teal, cyan-green |
| **Raw Sunsteel** | Bright orange-gold |
| **Raw Starsteel** | Blue-and-pink spiked crystal |
| **Raw Froststeel** | Solid deep blue |
| **Raw Mystical Molten Alloy** | Molten red-orange |

### Tier 2 — Advanced Refinery, level 15

**Raw Bloodsteel** (oxblood) · **Raw Embersteel** (orange) · **Raw Cindersteel** (ashier reddish-orange) · **Raw Crownsteel** (purple and gold) · **Raw Faesteel** (pink and purple plating) · **Raw Crystallized Gold** (gold shot through with crystal)

### Tier 3 — Supreme Refinery, level 30

**Raw Skysteel** (sky blue) · **Raw Aqualite** (bright cyan) · **Raw Amethril** (bright purple) · **Raw Verdanite** (green) · **Raw Venomsteel** (green and purple) · **Raw Infected Steel** (sickly grey-brown)

### Tier 4 — Master Refinery, level 45

**Raw Bubble Steel** (pale mint) · **Raw Pearly Steel** (iridescent off-white) · **Raw Thornsteel** (jagged olive) · **Raw Soulsteel** (violet spirit-metal) · **Raw Mechanite** (brushed steel-blue) · **Raw Nebula Steel** (deep violet with starlight) · **Raw Dragonsteel** (dark burnt red) · **Raw Matrix** (near-black with a shifting grid)

**Dragonsteel** and **Matrix** are the two best metals in the game.

## Ingots and Big Ingots

Every metal exists in two forms, and the difference decides what you can craft.

**Ingots** are the standard bar, straight from a refinery.

**Big Ingots** are made at the Basic Refinery: **6 ingots + 2 Ember Stones → 1 Big Ingot**. A Big Ingot is always exactly **one full rarity above** its small version.

!!! tip "Big Ingots are the key to \"+\" gear"
    Normal gear is crafted from small ingots in bulk — 30 to 42 per item. **"+" rarity gear is crafted from Big Ingots**, only two to five per item. If you want plus-tier equipment, you need Big Ingots.

| Metals | Ingot | Big Ingot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nightsteel, Abyssium, Sunsteel, Starsteel, Froststeel, Mystical Molten Alloy | Common | Uncommon |
| Bloodsteel, Embersteel, Cindersteel, Crownsteel, Faesteel, Crystallized Gold | Uncommon | Rare |
| Skysteel, Aqualite, Amethril, Verdanite, Venomsteel, Infected Steel | Rare | Epic |
| Bubble Steel, Pearly Steel, Thornsteel, Soulsteel, Mechanite, Nebula Steel | Epic | Legendary |
| **Dragonsteel, Matrix** | **Legendary** | **Unique** |

## Reagents

Every refining recipe burns a reagent, and they form a strict chain — which means **Ember Stone never becomes obsolete**.

| Reagent | Made at | Recipe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ember Stone** | Basic Refinery | 2 Cobblestone + 2 Coal → 4 |
| **Blaze Core** | Advanced Refinery | 1 Ember Stone + 1 Blaze Rod + 1 Coal → 8 |
| **Fire Essence** | Supreme Refinery | 2 Ember Stone + 2 Blaze Core + 2 Coal Block → 8 |

Ember Stone also drops from Resource Drops and Temple chests.

### Dungeon reagents

These come from fighting, not mining, and they're what gate gem and enchant crafting:

| Reagent | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Teleport Dust, Deep Pearl, Void Crystal** | End Striders and Enderlings |
| **Common Mana Stone** | Temple |
| **Scorched Bone** | Temple |
| **Dark Soul Sand** | Haunted — Cultists and Berserks |
| **Smoldering Ash** | Haunted — Monsters and Liches |

Manastones run their own ladder from Common all the way to Unique.

## The rarity ladder

**Common → Common+ → Uncommon → Uncommon+ → Rare → Rare+ → Epic → Epic+ → Legendary → Legendary+**

| Tier | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Common and Common+ | Gray |
| Uncommon and Uncommon+ | Green |
| Rare and Rare+ | Dark Aqua |
| Epic and Epic+ | Purple |
| Legendary and Legendary+ | Gold |

**What "+" actually means:** a "+" item has the same colour and the same number of stat rolls as its base tier, but each stat's magnitude sits about **30% of the way toward the next full tier**. A Rare+ sword genuinely beats a Rare one — but still loses to an Epic.

!!! tip "Break down \"+\" gear, not base gear"
    Each "+" tier **deconstructs into the essence of the tier above it**. Deconstructing a Rare+ weapon gives you an *Epic* Weapon Essence. That makes "+" gear far more efficient to break down than to sell.

**Unique** exists above Legendary, in red, and is currently reserved for the very best Big Ingots.

## Other materials

- **Weapon Essences** — from deconstructing gear. A failed deconstruct instead gives **Broken Weapon Powder**, and higher tiers lose more of it.
- **Soulbinders and Soulcrushers** — Apprentice, Intermediate and Arcane grades. Soulbinders bind an item to you (70%, 80% or 90% success); Soulcrushers break a soulbind.
- **Scroll of Identification** — 2 Paper + 1 Diamond. Identifies unidentified drops.
- **Gem Remover** and **Skin Remover** — shears that strip a gem or skin off an item.
- **Restoration Gem** — repairs magic items.
