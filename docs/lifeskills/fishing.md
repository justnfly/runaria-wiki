# Fishing

Fishing is a skill game, not a waiting game. Where you fish decides what bites *and* which minigame you have to beat.

## Quality grades

Every species comes in three grades, mirroring crops:

| Grade | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | Base size and value |
| ⭐ **Silver Star** | Bigger, worth more — **harder minigame** |
| ⭐ **Golden Star** | Biggest and most valuable — **hardest minigame** |

Gear that boosts star odds also makes every catch more demanding. That's the trade.

## What's biting, and where

**Oceans and saltwater** — Tuna, Pike, Sardine, Octopus, Sunfish, Red Snapper, and Blue and Pink Jellyfish. Ocean water also turns up kelp, seagrass, and occasional **Apple and Carrot Crates** full of loot. Junk is *less* common at sea than in fresh water.

**Rivers and lakes** — Goldfish, Perch, Mullet, Carp and Catfish. Fresh water is also the only place the **Rainbow Fish** appears, and it comes with seven bespoke minigames of its own.

**Swamps and mangroves** — Woodskip.

**Underground, below Y 0** — **Sturgeon**, the most valuable fish line in the game.

**Lava** — needs the Bone Rod. Overworld lava gives stone, flint, obsidian and rare diamonds; Nether lava gives netherrack, obsidian, gold and magma cubes, plus the **Void Salmon**, the only fish that lives in lava.

!!! tip "Fishing for skeletons"
    Holding the **Bone Rod** lets you hook **Skeletons** out of water at night, and **Wither Skeletons** out of lava.

## Rods

| Rod | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Beginner's** | Longer waits but a much easier minigame — the training rod |
| **Silver** | Big boost to Silver Star odds |
| **Golden** | Boost to Golden Star odds |
| **Star** | Adds 15 seconds of minigame time |
| **Bone** | Fish in lava, and attract skeletons |
| **Magical** | Catches **enchanted books** instead of fish. Needs a Book as bait and 30 experience levels, and burns a level per cast |
| **Master's** | Shortest wait, hardest minigame, boosts both star grades — the endgame rod |

A plain vanilla rod works, but with 50% longer waits and no bonuses.

## Baits, hooks and gear

| Item | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Simple Lures** | Noticeably easier minigame |
| **Magnetic Lures** | Faster bites, and 2 extra seconds |
| **Wild Lures** | Harder and 2 seconds shorter — but **+50% fish size** |
| **Delicate Hook** | Small boost to both star grades |

There's a set of fishing armour too: **Angler's Helmet** (easier minigame), **Deep Sea Chestplate** (Silver boost), **Swift Leggings** (shorter waits) and **Sturdy Boots** (shorter minigame).

**Luck of the Sea** is the single biggest lever on star quality. **Lure** only helps when fishing in lava.

## The minigames

| Where | Minigame |
| --- | --- |
| Ocean | **Tension** — keep the line in the safe band |
| Rivers and lakes | **Accurate Click Bar** — nine layout variants |
| Lava | **Hold** |
| Rainbow Fish | Seven unique minigames |

Each has Easy, Normal and Hard versions. **Silver fish skew toward Normal, Golden fish skew toward Hard.**

Bedrock players are allowed to skip the minigame, since touch input isn't a fair match for mouse. Auto-fishing is disabled for everyone.

## Beyond catching

- **Fishing Bag** — a personal container for rods, baits and hooks. With the right rank, catches deposit into it automatically.
- **Fish Market** — sell your catch. There's a **daily earnings cap of 10,000 coins**, so it's a steady income rather than a gold rush. Sturgeon pays best by a wide margin.
- **Size records** — the server tracks your personal best for every species and announces new records.
- **Fish Finder** — a handheld scanner that lists what's catchable in the water around you.

### Totems

Multi-block structures you build and then activate:

- **Double Loot Totem** — doubles your catches in an 8-block radius for 5 minutes. Activate it by holding a **Nautilus Shell**.
- **Golden Star Totem** — boosts Golden Star odds in an area.

Different totem types stack with each other. Two of the same type do not.

### Weekend competitions

Every **Saturday and Sunday at 09:30, 14:30 and 20:00**, a 5-minute competition fires if at least two players are around. The goal is simply **most fish caught**, and everyone gets 15% faster bites while it runs.

Prizes are 200 coins for first, 100 for second and third, and 10 for taking part. A boss bar shows the clock, your rank and who's leading.
