    The Menu¶

    Every dish in Runaria — 120 of them — and exactly what each one does.

    This page deliberately lists no ingredients. Finding those out is what the Recipe Book is for.

    New to cooking?

    Talk to Kate in Sylari. She'll set you up with a station and point you at your first recipes.

    These are base values

    Every figure below is the dish at Cooking level 1, made with ordinary produce — the floor, not the ceiling. Your own plate is stronger: effects scale with the cook's level and with the star quality of what went in, and the item's own lore always shows the real numbers for that plate. See Cooking.

    Prepared ingredients¶

    Four things made at a station aren't dishes — they're components other recipes ask for: Croissant Dough, Pancake Batter, Waffle Batter and Tomato Paste. You'll meet them as soon as you start baking.

    Quality carries through

    A batter made from starred produce stays starred, and passes that on to whatever you bake with it. See Cooking.
